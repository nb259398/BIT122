Cavendish University Website, designed as a comprehensive E-Learning Management System.Cavendish University Website, designed as a comprehensive E-Learning Management System. The platform addresses challenges such as school inaccessibility, transaction delays, scattered information, and low student engagement. By centralizing academic resources, announcements, and secure transactions, the website ensures timely access to information and fosters stronger interaction between students, faculty, and the wider community. It will serve as a digital hub for learning, collaboration, and communication, enhancing transparency and efficiency.
